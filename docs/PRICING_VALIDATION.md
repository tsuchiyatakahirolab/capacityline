# CapacityLine global pricing validation

Validated 2026-08-09 against publicly available vendor pages. Prices can change; Stripe remains the source of truth once a price is configured.

## Decision

Offer the **Founding Private Pilot at $499/month**, excluding tax, with no setup fee and monthly cancellation:

- one isolated recovery workspace;
- 10 governed recovery runs per month;
- up to five approved suppliers per run;
- custom recovery brief and deterministic eight-guardrail policy;
- Evidence Pack JSON and commitment-matrix CSV;
- recipient allow-list and launch-authority setup;
- founder-led onboarding and monthly outcome review.

Keep the public interactive product at $0 because it is a deterministic, zero-call proof. Quote Enterprise separately for SSO, roles, ERP integration, durable multi-site data, regional policies, or larger volumes.

## Is $499 defensible today?

Yes, but only as a **managed founding pilot**, not as a self-serve SaaS license. At the same public starting price, Precoro Core offers a much broader persistent procurement suite. CapacityLine earns the same monthly amount through specialization and service:

1. configure the customer's recovery policy and approved supplier scope;
2. enforce billing, authorization, consent-reference, jurisdiction-review, and recipient allow-list gates;
3. normalize live capacity commitments across eight decision fields;
4. preserve transcript evidence and ambiguous answers rather than inferring them;
5. deliver a human-reviewable fallback and Evidence Pack;
6. review the operating result with the customer each month.

The price is not defensible if onboarding, policy configuration, outcome review, or live governance is removed. A future self-serve tier should be lower-priced or materially broader.

## Commercial claim boundary

- Do not market CapacityLine as a cold-calling, telemarketing, or sales automation product.
- Do not claim universal legal compliance; regional rules depend on purpose, consent, recipient, time, disclosure, and recording practice.
- Do not claim avoided downtime or savings from the customer's modeled exposure.
- Do not claim GA, multi-tenancy, SSO, ERP write-back, or security certifications before those capabilities exist.
- Do say: operational supplier recovery, approved contacts, comparable commitments, evidence-backed recommendation, and human authority.

## Expansion trigger

Move beyond the $499 founding cohort only after pilots show repeat use and at least one of these measurable outcomes: lower time-to-qualified-fallback, fewer manual buyer touches, greater commitment completeness, or better promise-to-delivery calibration after ERP reconciliation.
